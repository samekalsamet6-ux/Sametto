**App name (30):** Aquarium World: Breed & Find

**Short description (80):**
Breed fish, crack genetics, design your dream tank, complete your collection!

**Full description:**
Aquarium World is a calm but deep fish-breeding game. Take over your grandfather's neglected little aquarium shop; pair your fish, predict which colours and tail shapes the fry will inherit, design your tank and become a famous breeder.

🐠 BREED FISH, DECODE GENETICS
• Guppies, mollies, endlers, platies, swordtails, bettas, shrimp and snails
• Inheritance inspired by real Mendelian genetics: dominant/recessive genes, sex-linked colours, mutations and rare surprises
• Develop true-breeding lines and discover hidden variations

🌿 DESIGN YOUR TANK
• Place plants, rocks, driftwood, substrate and decor; balance the water with light, filter, CO₂ and heater
• Follow the nitrogen cycle, water values and your fish's health
• Build several tanks and move fish between them
• Lush, smooth, living underwater scenes and relaxing music

📖 ENCYCLOPEDIA AND TRAIT BOOK
• Every variation and trait you find (tail shape, tail colour, body colour, pattern, size) is recorded
• Fry with mixed traits count towards your collection too

🏆 CONTESTS AND PROGRESS
• Aquascape and fish contests, orders, market and travelling merchant, season rewards, achievements and story chapters

🤝 SHARE WITH FRIENDS
• Share your profile card as a code and add friends' cards to your collection. No account needed.

🎬 OPTIONAL REWARDED ADS
• Watch a short ad only if you want to, for a temporary boost. No forced ads; can be turned off entirely in Settings.

Plays offline, no account required, no purchases. Suitable for kids and adults.
